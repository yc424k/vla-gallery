Key features
Removes N1.5's 4-layer adapter after the VLM, unfreezes the top 4 VLM layers, and doubles the DiT (32 layers). Combined with Decoupled WBC (RL lower body·IK upper body) for Unitree G1 whole-body loco-manipulation.
