Notes

Key features
A Mixture-of-Transformers inserting a panorama expert into π0.5 fuses global spatial context from a 360° panorama with local camera views, and collects wheeled bimanual robot data via single VR whole-body teleoperation. On 4 real tasks it reached an average stage completion of 91.3%·end-to-end success of 73.4%, well ahead of π0.5 (58.6%·30.0%).
Notes
Teleoperation: VR headset, two hand controllers, 3 waist/ankle trackers → SMPL reconstruction → GMR retargeting (base is a 3-DoF planar virtual floating base). π0.5 checkpoint name, total parameters, denoising steps, and training time not disclosed. The paper does not mention releasing code, data, or weights. Based on: π0.5: a Vision-Language-Action Model with Open-World Generalization (arXiv 2504.16054) — built by inserting a panoramic expert into the π0.5 dual-expert backbone
