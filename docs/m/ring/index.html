Key features
Builds a shape-agnostic indoor object search policy by imitation learning on expert trajectories of 1 million embodiments with randomized camera position·field of view·collider size etc. in sim, then RL fine-tuning with DD-PPO. Recorded 72.1% average success rate over 5 sim embodiments and 78.9% zero-shot on 4 real platforms including Stretch RE-1·LoCoBot·Go1.
