Notes

Key features
Blocks action-expert gradients from flowing into the VLM to preserve VLM knowledge while learning representations with FAST tokens. π0 needed 7.5× more steps to reach similar performance, and this method adds about 20% compute per step.
Notes
'π0.5 + KI' is a task-assigned label; the paper calls it 'knowledge insulation'. openpi's π0.5 and pi05_droid are trained with KI. Appendix B num_heads=18 conflicts with 8 in openpi. π0-FAST takes 2× longer execution time on the drawer task.
