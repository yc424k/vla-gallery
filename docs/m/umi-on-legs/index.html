Notes

Key features
A diffusion manipulation policy trained on UMI handheld demonstrations outputs task-frame end-effector trajectories, which a manipulation-centric whole-body controller (WBC) trained with sim RL tracks to execute on a quadruped + arm. A public UMI policy for a fixed arm was ported to the quadruped without modification, and throwing·pushing·cup rearrangement all achieved 70%+ real-robot success.
Notes
WBC GPU·number of parallel environments·training time not stated in paper (README: based on legged_gym PPO·IsaacGym); CLIP encoder variant not stated (original UMI paper cup policy is ViT-B/16); code·WBC checkpoint·trajectory data released, release of throwing·pushing DP checkpoints unverified; CoRL 2024. Based on: Universal Manipulation Interface: In-The-Wild Robot Teaching Without In-The-Wild Robots (arXiv 2402.10329) — extends UMI demonstration data·policy·code to a quadruped platform (stated in paper·README)
