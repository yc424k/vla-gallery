Notes

Key features
A two-stage VLA pretrained on 100K+ hours of UMI trajectories with VLM auto-labels (scene state-transition descriptions), then aligned to robots and instructions with about 10K hours of cross-embodiment data. Zero-shot performance in unseen environments rises with pretraining data and model scale, reaching RoboCasa365 57.4% and a RoboDojo score of 20.07, above the prior best (46.6%·13.07).
Notes
Pretraining objective is flow matching + Choice Policies regression + 0.1×NTP (VL:UMI = 1:9); the DiT is blocked from seeing the VLM's action-token KV to cut the copying shortcut; state-transition captions auto-generated with Qwen3.5-27B; 10+ minute room-scale suitcase-packing demo; code·checkpoints released 2026-08-03 (Apache-2.0, HF Xiaomi-Robotics-1-5B model_states.pt 10.2 GB, per-benchmark RoboCasa·RoboCasa365·VLABench checkpoints), 2B·10B not released, pretraining UMI data not released; arXiv v2 2026-07-22. Predecessor: Xiaomi-Robotics-0 (arXiv 2602.12684) — inherits the asynchronous training recipe and VL data (stated in paper)
