Notes

Key features
Freezes the VLM in both pre- and post-training and adds a FLARE (future latent representation alignment) loss (coefficient 0.2) to flow matching, learning also from human egocentric video. Generalizes to new motions beyond teleoperation via DreamGen neural trajectories.
