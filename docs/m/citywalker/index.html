Notes

Key features
Imitation-learns a GPS-goal-conditioned urban navigation policy from thousands of hours of YouTube city walking·driving videos labeled with trajectories via DPVO visual odometry. Trained on 1,000+ h, it outperformed fine-tuned ViNT zero-shot, and after 6 h of teleoperation fine-tuning reached a 77.3% real-world success rate on Go1 (fine-tuned ViNT 57.1%).
