Notes

Key features
Training-free hierarchical navigation policy that feeds an entire environment demonstration tour video into a long-context VLM (Gemini 1.5 Pro) to find the goal frame matching text/image instructions, and plans low-level waypoints on a topological graph built from the same tour. In an 836 m² real office it reports goal-finding success of 86% and 90% for reasoning-required and multimodal instructions, 26% and 60% higher than prior methods.
Notes
86% · 90% are goal-finding rates (Table 3); end-to-end success (Table 2) is 80% for reasoning-required · 85% multimodal, differing from the narrative; architecture per the MoE Transformer description in the Gemini 1.5 report; Gemini parameters and inference hardware not disclosed; code and weights not released.
