Architecture (original summary)
Stable Diffusion VAE latent + CDiT-XL (1B): 4 past frames of context, generates future frames with AdaLN conditioning on the sum of action (translation·yaw)·time shift k·diffusion step embeddings (250 denoising steps, 6 when distilled); planning: optimizes 8-step action sequences with CEM (N=120 candidates) or ranks NoMaD samples

Notes

Key features
Trains a 1B conditional diffusion transformer (CDiT) world model on robot driving video and actions to imagine future video for action sequences, planning·scoring trajectories by similarity to the goal image. On RECON, standalone planning was more accurate (ATE 1.13·RPE 0.35) than NoMaD (1.93·0.52), and adding unlabeled Ego4D video improved prediction in unseen environments.
Notes
