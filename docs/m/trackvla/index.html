Notes

Key features
An embodied visual tracking VLA that places a language head for target recognition and an anchor-based diffusion head for trajectory planning in parallel on a shared LLM, jointly learning recognition and tracking trajectory planning. Trained on its own EVT-Bench sim tracking data and 1.7M recognition samples, it showed robust tracking at 10 FPS in zero-shot Gym-UnrealCV and the real world.
Notes
Tracking sample count inconsistent in the text (855K vs 885K); TrackVLA weights not released (only EVT-Bench and Uni-NaVid evaluation code and weights released); EVA-CLIP and Vicuna-7B initialized from pretrained weights; CoRL 2025.
