Notes

Key features
A structurally native VLA that feeds pixels directly into the backbone token space through a patch embedder without a separate pretrained vision encoder, with a flow matching action expert directly reading KV from backbone layers at multiple depths. Under the same action expert·data conditions it beats Qwen3-VL-2B·Gemma-3-4B backbones on LIBERO (frozen backbone 90.9% vs 79.5%·84.5%), and reports LIBERO-plus 89.7·RoboTwin 2.0 Clean2Rand 69.40, the best among models with released code·weights.
