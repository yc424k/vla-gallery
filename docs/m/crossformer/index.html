Notes

Key features
Trains a single 130M Transformer on 900K trajectories from 20 embodiments without aligning observation or action spaces, controlling single and bimanual arms, wheeled robots, quadrupeds and quadcopters. Average success over 6 robots was 73%, higher than single-robot policies (67%) and the previous best cross-embodiment method (51%).
Notes
Training time inconsistent: 47h in the text and README vs 80h in Appendix B. Number of embodiments inconsistent: 20 in the text vs 30 in Figure 1. Go1 data are rollouts of sim RL expert policies. Language encoder not specified in the paper (official config uses Universal Sentence Encoder). HF checkpoint 519MB (300k steps), MIT license. LoCoBot navigation is conditioned on a topological graph and goal images. Total data hours not stated. Based on: Octo: An Open-Source Generalist Robot Policy (arXiv 2405.12213) — based on the Octo codebase (stated in README)
