Notes

Key features
A think-in-control VLA in which a fast action policy controls asynchronously, conditioned on the delayed state from slow VLM inference and explicit latency information (inference·elapsed time, ego-motion). Injects 0–10 s inference delays during training and fine-tunes with PPO, maintaining 10 Hz control even with multi-second delays and reaching DynaNav SR 55.29%, ahead of prior VLAs (NaVILA 28.24%).
Notes
