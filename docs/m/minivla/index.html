Key features
Shrinks OpenVLA from 7B to about 1B and adds VQ action chunks and history input. Runs at 12.5 Hz on L40s, 2.5× faster than OpenVLA (5 Hz).
Notes
With no arXiv paper, the Stanford AI Lab blog (2024-12-12) was used as the primary source. 2-frame history input gave about 5% improvement.
