Notes

Key features
Trains a 31M Transformer policy on multi-robot driving data that extends the GNM data mix, and adapts by replacing the goal token with soft prompts for other goal modalities such as GPS coordinates and route commands. Combining diffusion subgoal proposals with graph search, it performed kilometer-scale exploration and also worked zero-shot on a Go1 quadruped and LoCoBot unseen in training.
Notes
